
# Thrill Pass
### Find an adventure. Book the excitement.

## Project Overview

Thrill Pass is a proposed adventure discovery and booking platform designed to help people find, compare, and book recreational experiences in New Jersey and nearby New York.

Activities may include:
- ATV rides
- Ziplining
- Escape rooms
- Other indoor and outdoor adventures

## Project Purpose

The purpose of this project is to explore how a digital platform can make discovering and booking recreational adventures easier and more convenient.

## Target Audience

Our proposed audience includes:
- Individuals seeking exciting recreational activities.
- Friends and groups planning adventures.
- People organizing birthdays and special outings.

These audience descriptions are initial hypotheses to be investigated through research, not verified customer findings.

## Research Focus

Our research will examine:
- Three existing websites offering similar or related services.
- Two possible business approaches.
- Two customer persona hypotheses.
- The target audience.
- Potential website features and design decisions.

## Initial Geographic Focus

New Jersey and nearby New York, with the possibility of future expansion.
