## 1. Two Business Positions

### Option A: Budget-Friendly Adventures

Thrill Pass could focus on college students and young adults looking for affordable activities. Customers could compare prices, find group deals, and discover exciting experiences without spending too much money.

### Option B: Premium Adventure Experiences

Thrill Pass could focus on customers looking for unique experiences, such as ATV riding, ziplining, and escape rooms. This approach would emphasize quality, convenience, and memorable experiences rather than just low prices.

### Recommended Direction

We recommend starting with affordable and exciting adventures in New Jersey and nearby New York. This approach could attract students, young adults, and groups planning weekend outings.

## 2. Customer Persona Hypotheses

### Persona 1: Maya — College Student

- **Age:** 21
- **Location:** New Jersey
- **Background:** A college student who enjoys trying new activities with friends.
- **Goals:** Find affordable adventures, compare prices, and plan weekend activities.
- **Frustrations:** Hidden fees, confusing booking processes, and having to compare several websites.
- **Website Needs:** Price filters, group deals, reviews, and simple booking.

### Persona 2: Jordan — Weekend Adventure Planner

- **Age:** 28
- **Location:** New York
- **Background:** A working professional who enjoys exciting weekend experiences.
- **Goals:** Discover new activities, find convenient schedules, and book experiences quickly.
- **Frustrations:** Unclear availability, limited information, and confusing cancellation policies.
- **Website Needs:** Availability details, activity descriptions, reviews, and cancellation information.

These personas are fictional hypotheses, not actual interviewed customers.

## 3. Target Audience

Thrill Pass will initially focus on college students, young adults, and groups looking for recreational experiences in New Jersey and nearby New York.

Our proposed primary target audience is adults ages 18–35 who enjoy ATV riding, ziplining, escape rooms, and other indoor or outdoor adventures.

Our secondary audience includes couples, families, and people organizing birthdays or special outings.

These audience assumptions should be evaluated through research.

## 4. Connecting Research to Website Features

| Customer Need | Proposed Feature | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| Find nearby adventures | Location search | Helps users discover local activities |
| Compare affordable options | Price filters | Makes planning easier |
| Understand an activity | Photos and descriptions | Helps users know what to expect |
| Find reliable experiences | Customer reviews | Supports informed decisions |
| Plan with friends | Group booking information | Helps organize group outings |
| Avoid booking confusion | Availability and cancellation details | Clarifies booking expectations |
| Browse on mobile | Mobile-friendly layout | Makes finding activities convenient |

These are proposed features that should be checked against competitor research and user feedback.

## 5. Conclusion

Thrill Pass aims to make finding and comparing adventures easier. Instead of visiting multiple websites, customers could explore different activities and choose experiences that match their interests and budgets.

Our recommended direction is to focus initially on affordable adventures in New Jersey and nearby New York.
