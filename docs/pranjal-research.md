## 1. Two Business Positions

### Option A: Budget-Friendly Adventures

Thrill Pass could focus on college students and young adults looking for affordable activities. Customers could compare prices, find group deals, and discover exciting experiences without spending too much money.

**Potential advantage:** This could attract students and budget-conscious customers.

**Potential limitation:** Some adventure activities may be expensive, making it difficult to offer consistently low prices.

### Option B: Premium Adventure Experiences

Thrill Pass could focus on customers looking for unique experiences, such as ATV riding, ziplining, and escape rooms. This approach would emphasize quality, convenience, and memorable activities rather than just low prices.

**Potential advantage:** It could appeal to customers looking for special experiences.

**Potential limitation:** Higher prices may discourage budget-conscious customers.

### Recommended Direction

Thrill Pass will initially focus on ATV rides, ziplining, escape rooms, and related adventures in New Jersey and nearby New York. The platform will help users compare activities, prices, and available booking information in one place.

This direction matches our team's proposed focus on a specialized adventure-discovery platform.

## 2. Customer Persona Hypotheses

### Persona 1: Maya — College Student

**Age:** 21  
**Location:** New Jersey  
**Background:** Maya is a college student who enjoys trying new activities with her friends.

**Goals:**
- Find affordable adventures nearby.
- Compare prices before booking.
- Plan fun weekend activities with friends.

**Frustrations:**
- Hidden fees.
- Confusing booking processes.
- Having to visit multiple websites to compare activities.

**Proposed Website Needs:**
- Price filters.
- Group booking information.
- Customer reviews.
- Simple booking instructions.

### Persona 2: Jordan — Weekend Adventure Planner

**Age:** 28  
**Location:** New York  
**Background:** Jordan is a working professional who enjoys exploring new activities during weekends.

**Goals:**
- Discover exciting adventure experiences.
- Find activities with convenient schedules.
- Compare options before booking.

**Frustrations:**
- Unclear availability.
- Limited activity information.
- Confusing cancellation policies.

**Proposed Website Needs:**
- Location and date filters.
- Detailed activity descriptions.
- Availability information.
- Clear cancellation policies.

**Note:** Both personas are fictional examples created to explore possible customer needs. They are not based on actual customer interviews.

## 3. Target Audience

Thrill Pass will initially focus on people interested in adventure activities in New Jersey and nearby New York.

### Primary Target Audience

Our proposed primary audience is young adults ages 18–35, including college students and working professionals.

These customers may be interested in activities such as ATV riding, ziplining, escape rooms, and other weekend adventures.

### Secondary Target Audience

Our secondary audience includes:
- Couples planning dates or weekend outings.
- Friends organizing group activities.
- Families looking for recreational experiences.
- People organizing birthdays and special occasions.

### Geographic Focus

Thrill Pass will initially focus on New Jersey and nearby New York.

Starting locally allows the project to focus on a specific market before considering expansion.

These target audience descriptions are preliminary hypotheses that require further research.

## 4. Connecting Research to Website Features

Our team's competitor research examined GetYourGuide, Viator, and Groupon.

The following proposed features connect those findings with the needs identified in our customer personas.

| Research or Customer Need | Proposed Website Feature | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| GetYourGuide uses search and activity categories | Homepage search and adventure categories | Helps users discover activities |
| Viator provides destination and date selection | Location and date filters | Helps customers find relevant experiences |
| Groupon displays local categories and pricing | Clear prices and comparison filters | Makes comparing activities easier |
| Maya wants affordable adventures | Price filters | Helps budget-conscious customers |
| Maya plans outings with friends | Group booking information | Makes group planning easier |
| Jordan wants convenient schedules | Availability information | Helps customers plan weekend activities |
| Jordan wants clear booking conditions | Cancellation and booking details | Reduces confusion |
| Customers may browse on their phones | Mobile-friendly design | Makes the platform convenient to use |

### Proposed Customer Experience

1. Visit the Thrill Pass homepage.
2. Search for an adventure or browse categories.
3. Choose a location and preferred date.
4. Compare activities, prices, and descriptions.
5. Review availability and booking conditions.
6. Follow the available booking instructions.

This workflow is a design proposal and has not yet been tested with customers.

## 5. Conclusion

Thrill Pass aims to make discovering and comparing adventure activities easier.

Our proposed approach focuses on ATV rides, ziplining, escape rooms, and related experiences in New Jersey and nearby New York.

By offering clear information, useful filters, and organized activity categories, Thrill Pass could help customers make more informed decisions.

The next step is to evaluate these ideas through peer feedback and additional research before finalizing the website design.
